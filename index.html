Production brief

Type a topic.
Get a cartoon short.

Storyforge writes a fact-checked script, then a small cast acts it out: thick outlines, stick limbs, props and sets drawn on the page. A voice, music and captions come with it. You download the MP4.

Frame

What happens when you roll

  1. Script written and fact-checked, then repaired if the model truncates it
  2. Cast walks, points, jumps and lip-flaps in a drawn cartoon set
  3. Voice recorded on your own device, with music and sound effects
  4. MP4 encoded in the browser. Nothing is posted anywhere
