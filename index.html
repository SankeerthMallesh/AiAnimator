What's new in Loomy 2.1

Characters that actually do things, and better-looking, smoother videos.

  • Nobody just stands there. Characters hold keys, maps, money, gold and flasks, press big red buttons, write, read, lift crates, sweep, spy through binoculars, panic, cheer, salute, clap and count cash, in Cartoons, 3D and Blocky.
  • They move. People walk onto their mark, run across the room and walk in from the side, and the people behind are busy too (on the phone, reading, writing).
  • The line picks the action. "He grabbed the key" shows the key in his hands; a quiet line shows what people do in that place (typing in an office, counting cash in a vault).
  • Better pictures. Proper indoor lighting (no more washed-out haze), solid background people, faces that match the mood, and close-ups that keep the whole head.
  • People sit on their seats (chairs, sofas, thrones, hay bales, stools), and desks or counters no longer hide who the story is about.
  • Smoother videos. Always 30 frames a second and never below 720p, even on slower computers.

New in 2.0, the biggest update yet:

  • 2.5D worlds. Flat cartoon people inside real 3D places built for every story: a prison cell, a cell block, a bank lobby and vault, mission control, a space station and its toilet, a cockpit, a courtroom, a hospital, a classroom, a kitchen, a diner, a mine tunnel, a battlefield trench and more (26 places, full of real objects).
  • The narrator is only a voice. Nobody on screen tells the story: the characters act it out while the narrator speaks.
  • They do things. Dig, sleep, eat, drink, type, call, carry the loot, sit, doze off, in outfits that fit (inmates, guards, astronauts, doctors, bosses, chefs) with headsets, cigars, shades, gold chains and headlamps.
  • Every line gets the right place. A man in jail shows a jail cell; the guards show the cell block; the heist moves to the vault. Each video builds its own variation, with people going about their business in the background.
  • The 3D tab too: the same places, actions and accessories for its 3D people.
  • Made in 7 minutes. A video under a minute is finished within 7 minutes: Loomy times your computer first and keeps the best quality that fits.
  • Fixed: Clips exports again.

New in 1.6:

  • A new look. Red and white everywhere, with Liquid Glass bars, menus and panels, and the red stickman icon.
  • Real voices with feeling. Pick from 14 real voices (George, Brian, Liam, Jessica, Lily…) or let Auto match your narrator. Very expressive adds real emotion, gasps, laughs and sighs.
  • Places and icons that fit the topic. War gets a battlefield and a sword, kings get a castle; plus a hospital, museum, stadium, kitchen, jungle and volcano. Topic icons are on for every video.
  • Blocky is now "In this town…". One strange rule, a hero, and an odd twist that changes what it means. 240 town ideas to start from.
  • Captions on every video, in every tab.
  • Fixed: thumbnails for 9:16 videos now show in full, with the frame picker, words and download.
  • Faster: videos keep making at full speed when you switch to another tab, and the voice reads more lines at once.

New in 1.55, the biggest upgrade yet:

  • Feedback with grades. Every video gets a letter grade, A+ to F, for its hook, curiosity, pacing, clarity, visuals, title, length and sound, with a tip for each. Not good enough? Press Fix it for me and the AI rewrites the weak parts and makes it again.
  • Narrators with character. The Storyteller, Hype Host, Secret Teller, 1940s Newsreel, Deadpan Comic, Professor, Movie Trailer and Your Bestie.
  • Music that screams curiosity. Mystery Box, Ticking Clock, Detective, Heartbeat, Wonder, Countdown and Lo-fi Curious.
  • 242 video ideas. Search them, browse 20 categories, shuffle, and tap one to start.
  • Faster videos. A Speed choice (Fast, Balanced, Best), lighter defaults and less waiting before the voice starts.
  • 9:16 thumbnails for Shorts, and a site that fits your phone, tablet and laptop.

New in 1.5:

  • The 3D town's characters, everywhere. Every Cartoons video now uses the town's people: shaded bodies, bold outlines, real faces and a range of skin tones, on any set and on any computer.
  • They act it out. They talk, stroll, point, sit, cheer and hold things in their hands, and their faces follow the story.

New in 1.4:

  • Close-ups of things. When a line is about a thing, the camera cuts to it floating big in the middle, glowing and sparkling.
  • Space-station rooms. A cabin with Earth in the window, mission control with a countdown, bunks and a cockpit, picked when the story is up there.
  • Big punchy captions. A new caption style with one or two big words at a time (Settings, Captions).

New in 1.3:

  • A glossier cartoon look. Light glows and sparkles, tilted camera angles on big moments, crew jumpsuits, lab coats, headsets and new faces: smug, stunned, sleepy and nervous.
  • Narration that stops the scroll. Videos open by talking to you ("If you…") and keep a question open, so people stay to the end.

New in 1.2:

  • Cartoon characters use their things. In Cartoons, people show, drop, spin, grow and break what they hold, when the story says so.
  • Auto look for Cartoons. Loomy picks the 3D cartoon town or painted sets for each topic, and only uses the town on computers that can draw it.
  • Long pauses tightened for you. After every export, long gaps in the voice are trimmed automatically. One click puts them back.
  • Chapters in the description. Long videos get a ready-to-paste chapter list, so YouTube shows chapters.

New in 1.1:

  • Cut parts out, right where you export. Drag across the sound bar to cut a lagging voice or a long pause, or let Loomy find the long pauses for you. Mark cuts while the video is still exporting.

New in 1.0:

  • Hooks in the first seconds. Every video opens on its most dramatic moment, a headline that teases the payoff, and a sound hit.

  • Long videos, in chapters. Up to 15 minutes: cartoon history, 3D explainers and game-world stories, each chapter with its own title card.
  • The right look for the topic. 3D picks drawn people for stories and AI pictures for places and history.
  • A better editor. Text, captions, music, speed, zoom and transitions, plus Edit on every finished video.
